# v0.9 — Expanded Blackstone Employment Chapter

Version 0.9 turns the opening employee stage into a complete first chapter:

**New Hire → Mine Worker → Senior Miner → Shift Lead → Buy Your Freedom → Independent Miner**

## Runtime and data

Employee progression lives in `games/miner-incremental/data/incremental.json`. Its `employment` contract defines ranks, promotion requirements, work assignments, company tools, wage shares, coworkers, story scenes, notices, and Miller dialogue. Runtime code reconciles those definitions into the local save instead of scattering rank thresholds through UI handlers.

The release clause remains hidden through the first three ranks. Completing the Shift Lead scene reveals the $5,000 objective. Confirming the purchase deducts the money once, saves a `walkout-pending` transaction, and only ends employment when The Walkout completes. A reload resumes the scene without another charge. Replays are visual-only.

## Tuned progression baseline

The deterministic balance audit uses the actual package configuration and no rare-event windfalls. Exact results may vary during normal play because deposits, rewards, critical hits, lottery outcomes, and player choices vary.

| Play style | Mine Worker | Senior Miner | Shift Lead / discovery | Cash at discovery | Buyout reached | Work after discovery |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Casual purchases | 38 deposits / level 3 | 89 / level 5 | 150 / level 9 | $118 | 431 deposits | 281 deposits |
| Efficient tools | 38 deposits / level 3 | 89 / level 5 | 150 / level 9 | $343 | 396 deposits | 246 deposits |
| Save focused | 38 deposits / level 3 | 89 / level 5 | 150 / level 9 | $943 | 364 deposits | 214 deposits |

The target price remains $5,000 because all three profiles discover it well before they can afford it, yet the better Shift Lead assignment closes the remaining gap without requiring thousands of additional deposits. Blackstone's retained value at discovery is about $10,611 in the deterministic baseline, compared with $943 total wages before personal purchases.

## Save migration

Save schema 9 adds employee rank, promotions, assignment, employee deposits, contract discovery, dialogue choices, completed and pending scenes, notices, history, and buyout transaction state.

- Existing independent/company v0.8 saves stay independent. The six Chapter 1 scenes become historical replay entries and their old paid amount is preserved.
- Existing employed v0.8 saves are reconciled to the closest rank supported by level, employee deposits, and Blackstone value.
- Migration never queues all missed scenes, so returning players do not receive a scene flood.
- New story scenes and the Walkout remain package-local and do not alter the existing local-save namespace.

## Approved cinematic artwork

All six Chapter 1 scenes use individually approved 16:9 illustrations. The optimized WebP assets live under `games/miner-incremental/assets/cinematics/`; each scene declares a validated package-relative `assetPath`, and the runtime falls back to a labeled placeholder if loading fails. The final approval state and original art requirements remain documented in `games/miner-incremental/data/cinematic-art-manifest.json`.
